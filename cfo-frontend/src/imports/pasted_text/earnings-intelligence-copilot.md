Prompt Title:
Design a modern enterprise SaaS web platform called “Earnings Intelligence Copilot” for CFOs and Finance Leadership
Prompt:
Create a polished, executive-grade, AI-powered enterprise web application called Earnings Intelligence Copilot. The platform helps CFOs prepare for quarterly earnings calls by consolidating historical earnings intelligence, predicting likely analyst/investor questions, recommending high-confidence responses, simulating Q&A, and learning from actual earnings calls over time.
The product should support 2 user roles / logins:
CFO / Finance Leadership Login
Admin / Strategy / Product Intelligence Login
The UI should feel like a premium enterprise intelligence platform used by CFOs, IR teams, FP&A leaders, and finance strategy teams.
Use a design style that is:
Modern enterprise SaaS
Clean, boardroom-ready, high-trust, minimal clutter
Dark-on-light or deep navy finance-themed palette
Premium dashboards, clear data hierarchy, elegant charts
Highly interactive with cards, tabs, AI panels, timelines, editable modules, and confidence indicators
INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
ROLE 1: CFO LOGIN
Design 2 main primary screens plus supporting components.
SCREEN 1: “Quarterly Intelligence Hub”
This is the CFO’s home dashboard and should summarize the last 8 quarters of earnings-related intelligence in one place.
Purpose
Help the CFO quickly review historical performance, earnings narratives, investor sentiment, and recurring analyst concerns before the upcoming call.
Layout
Create a dashboard with:
A. Header Area
Page title: Quarterly Intelligence Hub
Company selector
Quarter selector (e.g. Q1 FY26, Q4 FY25, etc.)
“Generate Prep Pack” button
“Open Simulator” button
B. Executive Snapshot Panel
Top row KPI cards:
Revenue trend
EBITDA trend
Guidance accuracy
Market sentiment
Analyst confidence index
High-risk narrative areas
C. Last 8 Quarters Timeline / Summary Strip
A horizontally scrollable or tabular visual of the last 8 quarters showing:
Quarter
Headline performance
Beat / Meet / Miss indicator
Sentiment color
Call difficulty score
Top investor concern
Link to drill-down
D. Earnings Call Summary Section
For each quarter, show:
Key highlights
Financial performance summary
Guidance given
Management commentary summary
Earnings call transcript summary (AI condensed)
Notable strategic themes (margin, demand, capex, pricing, etc.)
E. Questions Asked in Prior Calls
Interactive section showing:
Top analyst questions asked
Categorization of questions:
Revenue / Growth
Margin / Profitability
Guidance
Capital Allocation
Costs / Inflation
Region / Segment Performance
Competition
Regulation / Risk
Frequency of repeated questions across quarters
“Most likely to recur” tags
F. Market / External Chatter Panel
Show synthesized chatter from:
Analysts
Investors
Earnings previews
Media narratives
Peer commentary
Social / financial community sentiment
This should include:
Top themes
Positive vs negative tone
What the Street is focused on
What changed this quarter vs last quarter
G. Sentiment Meter
A visual sentiment meter for each quarter showing:
Analyst sentiment
Investor sentiment
Media sentiment
Management confidence perception
Use elegant gauges / trend lines / heatmaps.
H. Narrative Risk Heatmap
Add a very useful panel showing:
“Potential areas of vulnerability this quarter”
Examples:
Weak volume growth
Margin compression
Working capital deterioration
Capex concern
Guidance credibility
Segment underperformance
This should be a heatmap with severity and recurrence.
I. Recommended Focus Areas for CFO
AI-generated prep recommendations:
“Be ready to explain margin bridge”
“Expect questions on guidance conservatism”
“Analysts may probe inventory and cash conversion”
“Need sharper narrative on market share gains”
Use a right-side AI assistant panel.

Difficulty score
Analyst persona / type

Regenerate alternatives

When a question is clicked, show:
Recommended answer draft
Structured talking points
CFO-ready concise answer
Expanded IR-friendly answer
Supporting proof points
Numbers / evidence references
Suggested tone:
Confident
Cautious
Reassuring
Strategic

Allow the CFO to:

Red flag topics

PDF

Add a feature called:
“Answer Stress Test”
For any drafted answer, AI should show:
What analysts may ask next
Weaknesses in the answer
Ambiguity flags
Potential contradiction with prior quarter statements
Areas where answer may sound evasive
Risk of creating negative market interpretation
This is a very important premium feature and should be designed visually as a “Challenge Panel”.
G. Confidence & Evidence Layer
For every answer generated, show:
Confidence score
Evidence sources used
Internal consistency score
Historical consistency with prior quarters
Sensitivity / disclosure risk flag
H. Mock Q&A Mode (Optional but high value)
Add a simulation mode where:
AI asks questions one by one like an analyst
CFO can type or record an answer
AI evaluates:
Clarity
Confidence
Completeness
Market safety
Investor friendliness
This is a standout feature and should appear as:
“Live Rehearsal Mode”

ADDITIONAL CRITICAL FEATURES TO ELEVATE THE PLATFORM
Please include these as additional modules or expandable widgets across the product.
These are the pieces that will make the concept much more powerful for CFOs and finance leaders.
1. Analyst Persona Intelligence
Create a feature showing:
Which analysts usually ask what type of questions
Analyst aggressiveness / style
Historical themes by analyst
Likely analysts for next call
This is extremely useful in real preparation.
Example cards:
“Analyst A usually probes gross margin and guidance quality”
“Analyst B often asks about capital allocation and market share”
2. Peer Benchmark Lens
A comparison view showing:
What peers were asked in their latest earnings calls
Which topics are trending in the sector
What narratives investors are rewarding / punishing
This should help the CFO anticipate industry-wide question spillover.
3. Narrative Consistency Checker
A smart checker that flags:
If this quarter’s messaging contradicts prior quarter commentary
If guidance language has shifted materially
If the answer could trigger trust concerns
This is a very high-value executive safeguard.
4. Disclosure / Compliance Guardrails
Add a small but powerful control layer:
“Potential forward-looking statement risk”
“May need legal review”
“Possible selective disclosure concern”
“Unsupported claim detected”
This will make the product more enterprise-ready.
5. Board / IR / CEO Alignment Mode
Create a collaboration layer where:
CFO, CEO, IR Head can align on the final narrative
Comments / approvals can be added
Final approved answer set can be locked
This makes it practical beyond just a demo.
6. Earnings Readiness Score
Create a prominent score such as:
“Earnings Readiness Index”
Calculated from:
Coverage of likely questions
Confidence of answers
Risk exposure
Historical alignment
Peer sensitivity
Narrative consistency
This gives leadership one number to rally around.
7. War Room View
Add an optional “Day of Earnings” mode showing:
Final approved Q&A pack
Key messages
Red topics to avoid
Investor watchpoints
Live note capture
This makes the platform operational, not just analytical.
